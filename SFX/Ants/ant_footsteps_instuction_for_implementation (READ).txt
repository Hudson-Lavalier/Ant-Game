#1 ANT WALKING
ant_footsteps_loop_01.ogg
ant_footsteps_loop_02.ogg

Playback:
- select random loop when movement begins
- random initial playback offset
- continuously loop while movement remains active
- stop when movement ends
- new random selection + offset on next movement period

#2 HEAVY ANT WALKING

- when heavy ant movement begins:
  - randomly choose loop 01 or 02
  - choose a random start offset
  - begin playback
- while the ant remains moving:
  - keep looping
- when movement stops:
  - stop the sound
- when movement starts again:
  - randomly choose a loop and random start offset again
